  • HOW IT WORKS

    Print on demand in India is a business model where products like T-shirts and hoodies are printed only after an order is placed. Qikink handles printing and shipping across India, so sellers never need to hold inventory. Qikink supports Prepaid and Cash on Delivery (COD) orders as well in India. the background video is just for advertisment

    You list custom products on your store. A customer orders, Qikink prints and ships directly to the customer under your brand. No inventory, no minimums and no upfront investment. Orders at Qikink are printed using state-of-the-art machinery to print a single garment at a time.

    Fulfillment & Shipping: All print on demand orders are shipped within 2 working days. AOP apparels are dispatched within 3-4 working days. The overall fulfillment time, including the delivery time, is 3-5 working days.

    Returns & Support: For damaged or defective items, simply raise a replacement request within 7 days of delivery along with an unboxing video and product photos. For size exchanges or no-defect returns, place a new order for your customer. For orders lost in transit, Qikink automatically issues a refund. Contact : primecanvastvm@gmail.com
